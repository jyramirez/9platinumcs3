## Part 1
**a. What is duck typing, as explained in the video?**

- As explained in the video, **duck typing** is another version of polymorphism. It does not involve inheritance, and instead it treats something like an object as a different as long as said object have the needed attribute and methods.

**b. What did the Car class need to have so it could work inside the same loop as Dog and Cat, even if it does not inherit from the Animal class?**

- The Car classes needed to have the 'speak' attribute. Even if it didn't inherit from the Animal class, once it got said attriute, it was able to work.
